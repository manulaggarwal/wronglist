# Pattern 5: Watch lists — deferring adoption on purpose

*The honest alternative to FOMO adoption and infinite deferral.*

## The mechanism

For every tool/practice you evaluate and DON'T adopt: record it with explicit
**revisit triggers** — measurable conditions under which you'll reconsider.

```markdown
| OpenSpace | skill lifecycle platform, 7.4k⭐ | v2 full rewrite | too heavy now |

Revisit when ANY fires:
- [ ] skill count > ~120
- [ ] >1/3 of skills ecosystem-installed, unverified
- [ ] platform stabilizes: no full rewrite for 6 months
- [ ] native alternative ships in the platform we already use
```

## Why it works

- **It converts "no" into "not yet, and here's the test."** No re-litigating
  the same tool every month on vibes.
- **It's future-proofing as adoptable-late.** Keep contracts strict and
  outcome data flowing (run logs); when a trigger fires, migration is a
  script, not a rebuild.
- **Triggers beat discipline.** You will not remember. The checkbox will.

## Nobody publishes these

Watch lists are private in most orgs (they read as indecision). Published,
they're a record of judgment: here's what we saw, here's our bar, here's
what would change our mind. That's the most falsifiable artifact in this
repo — you can check whether our triggers fired.
